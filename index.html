Function Key

Inner-product functional encryption · DDH (ABDP15) · ristretto255

Encrypt a vector, hand out a key that answers one question about it — the weighted sum <x, y> — then watch where that one answer runs out of room, and what happens once you have handed out enough of them.

WHY IT MATTERS

Releasing a statistic without releasing the records is the shape of almost every real data-sharing request, and functional encryption is the version of that with a proof attached rather than a promise. The catch is worth knowing first: the authority issuing those keys is deciding, one key at a time, how much of the plaintext it has already given away — and there is a point past which it has given away all of it.
